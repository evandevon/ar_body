Animate my drawing

Draw one character, take a photo, then a partner films you moving — and your drawing copies you.

  • Plain white paper, thick dark lines.
  • Stand it like a star: arms and legs apart, not touching the body.
  • Photograph it flat, in good light, filling most of the photo.
